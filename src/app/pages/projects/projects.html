Projects & Research

Li-Fi Based Vehicle Authentication System

Research Paper & Prototype · SRM TRP Engineering College

Developed a secure vehicle authentication system using Light Fidelity (Li-Fi) technology, focusing on optical data transmission for high-security environments.

  • Built prototype using Arduino UNO, laser diodes, and solar panel receivers
  • Implemented AES encryption for secure optical communication
  • Achieved 98% authentication accuracy in controlled environments
  • Explored applications in military, transportation, and high-security systems

EDU VISION – Automated Attendance System for Rural Schools

Smart India Hackathon 2025 · Team PROBOTS · PS ID: 25012

Designed a mobile-based attendance system using on-device AI to enable secure, offline student verification in rural schools with no cloud dependency.

  • Used FaceNet (TensorFlow Lite) to generate facial embeddings
  • Performed offline verification using cosine similarity
  • Built pipeline with ML Kit, Flutter, SQLite, and on-device inference
  • Focused on accessibility, reliability, and rural deployment constraints

PantryIQ – AI-Powered Food Inventory Assistant

Internal Ideathon Hackathon · 3rd Place · Tiruchirappalli

Ideated and presented an AI-based food inventory assistant designed to track groceries, predict expiry dates, and recommend recipes using multimodal machine learning.

  • Applied OCR, image recognition, and barcode scanning
  • Worked on receipt OCR and expiry prediction pipelines
  • Used TensorFlow Lite and Google ML Kit for on-device intelligence
  • Selected among high-potential ideas for future incubation
